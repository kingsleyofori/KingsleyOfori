# Hola!

**Land Economist · Construction Management Researcher · Digital Built Environment**

I am a researcher interested in how **data, computational modelling, and digital technologies** can be used to understand and solve problems in the built environment. My work sits across **construction, real estate, urban systems, and resilience**, with a growing focus on digital methods and evidence-based research.

I use computation not simply to analyse data, but to **model problems, test scenarios, and understand how built-environment systems behave under changing conditions.**

### Research interests

`Digital Built Environment` · `Construction Management` · `Computational Modelling`
 · `Construction Risk` · `Real Estate` · `Urban Systems`
